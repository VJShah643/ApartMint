# Student Housing in Uppsala: Complete Guide

## Why Student Housing Matters

Students in Uppsala have **unique housing challenges**:
- Temporary need (1-5 years)
- Limited income (CSN/part-time work)
- Urgent need (starting studies soon)
- Often first time renting independently

This guide covers ALL student housing options, from university corridors to private rentals.

## Option 1: Student Housing Organizations

### Studentstaden (Uppsala's Largest Student Housing)

**Who They Are**:
- Non-profit foundation
- Owned by Uppsala Student Union (Uppsala Studentkår)
- 11,000+ student rooms
- Largest student housing provider in Sweden

**Requirements**:
✅ Must be registered student at Uppsala University OR SLU
✅ Must have current term registration
✅ Valid study pace (at least 50% during term time)
✅ Valid housing queue days with Studentstaden

**Queue System**:
- Separate from Bostad Uppsala (different queue!)
- Earn queue days from registration
- Register at: studentstaden.se
- Cost: FREE to register

**Typical Wait Times**:
| Area | Room Type | Typical Queue Days Needed |
|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Flogsta | Corridor room | 0-50 days |
| Studentstaden center | Corridor room | 50-100 days |
| Rackarbergsgatan | 1-room apartment | 150-300 days |
| Ekeby | Shared apartment | 100-200 days |
| New buildings | 1-room apartment | 300-600 days |

**Rent Levels**:
- Corridor room: 3,500-4,500 kr/month
- Shared apartment room: 4,000-5,000 kr/month
- 1-room apartment: 5,500-7,500 kr/month
- Includes: Basic utilities (water, heating)
- Student internet often included

**Contract Terms**:
- Maximum 3 years total in student housing
- Can extend if continuing studies
- Must leave 6 months after graduation
- Can have multiple contracts (move between buildings)

**Application Process**:
1. Register on studentstaden.se
2. Start accumulating queue days
3. Browse available rooms weekly
4. Apply to rooms (unlimited applications)
5. Winner selected by queue days
6. Accept within 48 hours
7. Sign contract and move in

**Pros**:
✅ Cheaper than general market
✅ Designed for students (social, study spaces)
✅ Central locations near university
✅ Student community
✅ Fixed-term (know your contract length)
✅ Relatively easy to get (especially corridor rooms)

**Cons**:
❌ Maximum time limit (3 years)
❌ Must be actively studying
❌ Shared facilities in many buildings (corridor rooms)
❌ Strict rules (noise, parties)
❌ Must leave after graduation

**Strategy**:
1. Register IMMEDIATELY when accepted to university
2. Accept first offer even if not ideal
3. Keep applying while living there
4. Upgrade to better room within Studentstaden
5. Simultaneously register for Bostad Uppsala (for post-graduation)

## Option 3: Private Student Housing Companies

### Heimstaden Student Apartments

**What They Offer**:
- Some buildings designated "student-friendly"
- No queue system (apply directly)
- First-come, first-served with credit checks

**Requirements**:
✅ Proof of student status (enrollment certificate)
✅ Income: 3x rent (CSN counts!)
✅ No payment remarks
✅ References or guarantor

**Rent Levels**:
- 1-room: 7,000-9,000 kr/month
- Higher than student housing, lower than full market

**Student Advantages**:
- CSN (student aid) counts as income
- Parents can be guarantors
- More flexible on credit for students
- Faster than Bostad Uppsala queue

**Strategy**:
- Apply when you get CSN approval
- Have parents ready as guarantors
- Can get apartment within 1-3 weeks!

### Victoria Park, Wallenstam (Other Private)

**Similar to Heimstaden**:
- Direct application (no queue)
- Student-friendly policies
- CSN accepted
- Guarantor options
- Faster than first-hand queue

## Option 4: Nations (Uppsala Tradition)

### What Are Nations?

**Unique to Uppsala and Lund**:
- Student "nations" = historical student clubs
- Based on Swedish regions
- Social + housing functions
- 13 nations in Uppsala

**Nation Housing**:
- Some nations own apartment buildings
- Typically very cheap
- Strong community
- Often older buildings (charming but basic)

**How to Access Nation Housing**:

1. **Join a Nation**:
   - Membership required (300-500 kr/semester)
   - Choose based on region OR housing availability
   
2. **Check Housing Queues**:
   - Each nation has internal queue
   - Usually MUCH shorter than Bostad
   - Some nations: 0-6 months wait
   
3. **Apply Through Nation**:
   - Contact nation housing coordinator
   - Often informal process
   - Personal connections help

**Rent Levels**:
- Nation housing: 2,500-4,500 kr/month
- CHEAPEST option in Uppsala!
- Often shared apartments
- Basic facilities

**Best Nations for Housing**:
| Nation | Housing Reputation | Typical Wait |
|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| Gotlands Nation | Many apartments | Short |
| Norrlands Nation | Good housing stock | 3-6 months |
| Smålands Nation | Decent options | 6-12 months |
| Södermanlands-Nerikes | Some housing | Varies |
| Västgöta Nation | Limited but nice | Longer wait |

**Pros**:
✅ VERY cheap rent
✅ Strong student community
✅ Cultural experience (traditional Uppsala student life)
✅ Shorter queues than Bostad
✅ Flexible for students

**Cons**:
❌ Must join nation (membership fee)
❌ Often older buildings
❌ May be far from campus
❌ Shared facilities common
❌ Varying quality

**Strategy**:
- Join multiple nations
- Get on all housing queues
- Attend nation events (network!)
- Take first offer, upgrade later

## Option 5: Second-Hand Rentals

### What Is Second-Hand?

**Andrahandskontrakt** (Second-Hand Contract):
- You rent from someone who has first-hand contract
- Temporary (usually 3-12 months)
- Often furnished
- More expensive than first-hand

**Where to Find**:
- **Blocket.se**: Largest Swedish classifieds (housing section)
- **Facebook Groups**: "Uppsala Studentbostäder", "Uppsala Housing", etc.
- **University Notice Boards**: Physical + online
- **Qasa.se**: Second-hand rental platform
- **Samtrygg.se**: Second-hand with rental guarantees

**Typical Rent**:
- 1-room: 6,000-10,000 kr/month
- Often includes furniture, internet, utilities
- More than first-hand, but AVAILABLE

**Requirements**:
✅ Deposit (1-3 months rent, negotiable)
✅ Move-in date flexibility
✅ Sometimes: proof of student status

**Student Second-Hand Advantages**:
- Students often sublet when:
  - Going on exchange abroad
  - Summer internships
  - Graduating and want to keep contract for a few months
- Fellow students more understanding
- Flexible terms
- Social connections

**Risks & How to Avoid**:

⚠️ **Scams**:
- NEVER pay before seeing apartment
- NEVER send money abroad
- Always verify landlord's first-hand contract
- Meet in person

⚠️ **Illegal Subletting**:
- Landlord must approve second-hand contracts
- Get written permission
- Otherwise: risk eviction + legal issues

⚠️ **Overpricing**:
- Legal limit: Second-hand can't be MORE than 15% above first-hand rent
- Check hyresnämnden.se for rent guidelines
- Report illegal overcharging

**How to Succeed in Second-Hand Market**:

1. **Be Fast**: Respond to listings within HOURS
2. **Be Prepared**: Have documents ready (ID, enrollment certificate, references)
3. **Be Flexible**: Take short contracts to get IN
4. **Be Personal**: Write friendly, honest messages
5. **Network**: Tell everyone you need housing
6. **Start Early**: Begin searching 2-3 months before you need it

**Example Good Message**:

```
Hi! I'm a 22-year-old master's student starting at Uppsala University in August. 
I'm looking for a room/apartment for the fall semester (Aug-Jan). 
I'm clean, quiet, and responsible - I can provide references from previous landlords. 
I have CSN + part-time work income, can pay deposit immediately.
Would love to view the apartment! Can meet anytime this week.
Best regards,
[Your name] + [Phone]
```

## Option 7: Bostad Uppsala (First-Hand Queue)

### Can Students Use Bostad Uppsala?

**YES! Absolutely recommended**:
✅ Anyone 18+ can register
✅ No requirement to be student
✅ Register NOW even as student
✅ Build queue days while in student housing
✅ Perfect for POST-graduation housing

**Strategy for Students**:

**Year 1 (Student Housing)**:
- Register for Bostad Uppsala
- Live in Studentstaden/nation housing
- Accumulate queue days (365 after 1 year)

**Year 2-3 (Still Student)**:
- Continue student housing
- Keep earning Bostad queue days
- After 2 years: 730 queue days!
- After 3 years: 1,095 queue days!

**Year 4-5 (Graduating)**:
- Student housing kicks you out after graduation
- BUT: You have 1,000+ Bostad queue days
- Can move DIRECTLY to first-hand apartment
- No gap in housing!

**This is CRUCIAL**: Most students don't realize they should register for Bostad IMMEDIATELY when starting university. Don't make this mistake!

## Financial Planning for Students

### Understanding CSN (Student Aid)

**CSN Components**:
- **Grant**: ~3,300 kr/month (free money, don't repay)
- **Loan**: ~7,100 kr/month (must repay after graduation)
- **Total**: ~10,400 kr/month if you take full amount

**Housing Calculations**:

**Example Budget (Full CSN)**:
| Category | Amount | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Rent | 4,500 kr | Corridor room in Studentstaden |
| Food | 2,500 kr | Groceries, occasional eating out |
| Phone/Internet | 300 kr | Often included in student housing |
| Transport | 600 kr | UL student card |
| Books/Supplies | 500 kr | Varies by program |
| Social/Fun | 1,000 kr | Nations, activities |
| **Total** | **9,400 kr** | Leaves 1,000 kr for savings/unexpected |

**CSN as Income**:
- Heimstaden, Wallenstam: Accept CSN as income
- Private landlords: Often accept CSN
- Bostad Uppsala: No income requirement (queue-based)
- Student housing: No income requirement

**Income Rule Calculation**:
- Rent: 6,000 kr/month
- Need 3x: 18,000 kr/month income
- CSN: 10,400 kr/month
- **Shortfall**: 7,600 kr/month
- **Solution**: Guarantor (parent) OR part-time work

### Part-Time Work

**Typical Student Jobs**:
- Campus jobs: 8,000-12,000 kr/month (part-time)
- Retail: 10,000-15,000 kr/month (15-20 hrs/week)
- Tutoring: 200-300 kr/hour
- Research assistant: 150-200 kr/hour

**Income Combinations**:
- CSN (10,400 kr) + Part-time work (8,000 kr) = 18,400 kr/month
- Now can afford: 6,000 kr apartments (18,400 / 3 = 6,133 kr)

### Guarantor (Borgensman)

**What Is It?**:
- Someone (usually parent) guarantees your rent
- If you don't pay, they must pay
- Common for students

**Requirements**:
- Swedish resident (usually)
- Stable income (3-4x the rent)
- Good credit
- Willing to sign legal commitment

**When You Need It**:
- CSN not enough for 3x rent rule
- No work income yet
- First time renting
- Private landlords often require

**Strategy**:
- Ask parents BEFORE apartment hunting
- Get their income documents ready
- Have them available for quick signing

## Common Student Housing Mistakes

### Mistake #1: Not Registering for Bostad Early

**The Mistake**:
- "I'll just use student housing for 3 years, then figure it out"
- Don't register for Bostad Uppsala until final year

**The Problem**:
- Graduate with 0-365 Bostad queue days
- Can't get decent first-hand apartment
- Forced into expensive second-hand
- Years behind in queue

**The Fix**:
✅ Register for Bostad DAY ONE of university
✅ Build queue days while in student housing
✅ Graduate with 1,000-1,500 days = strong position

### Mistake #2: Being Too Picky Initially

**The Mistake**:
- "I'll only accept a 1-room in Centrum"
- Turn down corridor rooms, far suburbs

**The Problem**:
- Stay in temporary/expensive housing
- Lose time accumulating in proper housing
- Miss "ladder" opportunities

**The Fix**:
✅ Accept first reasonable offer
✅ Upgrade later with more queue days
✅ Get IN the system quickly

### Mistake #3: Not Networking

**The Mistake**:
- Only search online
- Don't tell people you need housing
- Skip nation events

**The Problem**:
- Miss word-of-mouth opportunities
- Nation housing has personal element
- Private landlords often prefer referrals

**The Fix**:
✅ Tell everyone you're looking
✅ Attend nations social events
✅ Join student Facebook groups
✅ Be visible and friendly

### Mistake #4: Falling for Scams

**The Mistake**:
- "Too good to be true" listings
- Pay deposit before viewing
- Wire money abroad

**The Problem**:
- Lose deposit (3,000-15,000 kr)
- No apartment
- Hard to recover money

**The Fix**:
✅ ALWAYS view in person
✅ Verify landlord identity
✅ Check first-hand contract
✅ Pay deposit ONLY after contract signed

### Mistake #5: Ignoring Nations

**The Mistake**:
- "Nations are just for partying"
- Don't join any nation
- Miss housing opportunities

**The Problem**:
- Nations have CHEAP housing
- Shorter queues than Bostad
- Strong community support

**The Fix**:
✅ Join at least one nation
✅ Get on housing queue immediately
✅ Attend events (network!)
✅ Leverage traditional Uppsala student system

## Summary: Best Student Housing Strategy

### The Ultimate Student Housing Plan

**Phase 1: Pre-Arrival** (Before starting studies)
1. Register for Bostad Uppsala immediately
2. Register for Studentstaden immediately
3. Join nation(s) and get on housing queues
4. Browse second-hand to understand market
5. Arrange guarantor with parents

**Phase 2: Initial Housing** (First semester)
1. Accept FIRST reasonable offer (student housing, second-hand, nation)
2. Goal: Be IN Uppsala with stable housing
3. Keep applying for upgrades
4. Build network

**Phase 3: Student Years** (Years 1-3)
1. Live in student/nation housing
2. Passively accumulate Bostad queue days
3. Upgrade within student system if possible
4. Enjoy university, don't stress housing

**Phase 4: Graduation Planning** (Final year)
1. Check Bostad queue days (should have 1,000-1,500)
2. Apply to first-hand apartments actively
3. Time move-out from student housing with Bostad win
4. Smooth transition to post-student life

**Outcome**:
✅ Always housed throughout studies
✅ Relatively cheap student rent
✅ Graduate with strong Bostad position
✅ Transition smoothly to first-hand apartment
✅ No housing gap or stress

**Final Tip**: The students who struggle with housing are those who wait until the last minute. The students who thrive are those who plan ahead, register early, and are flexible about their first apartment. Be the second type!
