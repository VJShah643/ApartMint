# Application Tips: Winning Your Apartment

## The Application Mindset

Getting an apartment in Uppsala requires strategy, preparation, and persistence. This guide covers everything from mindset to practical tactics for winning your application.

## Understanding the Selection Process

### Bostad Uppsala (First-Hand, Queue-Based)

**Selection Method**:
- **Automatic by queue days** (highest wins)
- NOT subjective (landlord doesn't "choose" you)
- Your application = expressing interest
- Winner determined automatically

**What This Means**:
- You can't "charm" your way in
- Your queue days are everything
- Multiple applications = multiple chances
- No penalty for applying widely

**Strategy Implications**:
✅ Apply to EVERYTHING you'd accept
✅ Check "Last offered to" to gauge chances
✅ Be ready to respond fast (48-hour acceptance window)
✅ Focus on accumulating queue days above all else

### Heimstaden (First-Hand, No Queue)

**Selection Method**:
- **First-come, first-served** with qualification checks
- Income requirement: 3x rent
- Credit check: No payment remarks
- References: Previous landlords, employer, character references
- Guarantor: If income insufficient

**What This Means**:
- Speed matters (apply within HOURS of listing)
- Your application quality matters
- Professional presentation helps
- Having documents ready is crucial

**Strategy Implications**:
✅ Set up notification alerts for new listings
✅ Have all documents ready (pre-prepared folder)
✅ Write professional, personable cover message
✅ Respond IMMEDIATELY when listing appears

### Private Landlords (First-Hand)

**Selection Method**:
- **Subjective choice** by landlord
- May consider: income, references, personality, "fit"
- Often informal process
- Personal connection can help

**What This Means**:
- Presentation matters A LOT
- Being likeable helps
- Referrals/networking gives advantage
- Landlord looking for "good tenant" not just qualified

**Strategy Implications**:
✅ Write friendly, personal message
✅ Present yourself as responsible, clean, quiet
✅ Leverage personal connections
✅ Be flexible on terms to seem accommodating

### Second-Hand Rentals

**Selection Method**:
- **Varies by landlord** (student subletting)
- Often prioritize: quick move-in, clean, responsible, shared interests
- Informal, personal

**What This Means**:
- Your message/personality matters most
- Fellow students sympathetic to other students
- Timing is everything (first responder often wins)

**Strategy Implications**:
✅ Respond within MINUTES if possible
✅ Be personable and relatable
✅ Emphasize shared status (student, similar age, etc.)
✅ Be flexible on move-in dates

## Application Preparation Checklist

### Document Folder (Always Ready)

**Identity Documents**:
☐ Copy of ID (passport or Swedish ID card)
☐ Personnummer certificate (if you have it)
☐ Contact information (email, phone, current address)

**Income Documentation**:
☐ Employment contract (if working)
☐ Last 3 months payslips
☐ Tax return (last year's tax declaration)
☐ Bank statements (last 3 months)
☐ CSN confirmation letter (if student - shows monthly amount)
☐ Employer reference letter (confirming position and salary)

**Study Documentation** (If Student):
☐ University enrollment certificate (Intyg om studier)
☐ Study pace confirmation (at least 50% for student housing)
☐ CSN decision letter (shows you qualify for student aid)

**Rental History**:
☐ Previous landlord reference letters
☐ Previous rental contracts (shows you've rented before)
☐ Move-out inspection reports (if available - shows you left in good condition)

**Character References**:
☐ 2-3 reference letters from:
  - Professors/teachers
  - Employers
  - Long-time acquaintances (not family)
☐ Contact information for references (name, phone, relationship)

**Financial Guarantor** (If Needed):
☐ Parent/guardian agreement to be guarantor
☐ Guarantor's income documentation (payslips, tax return)
☐ Guarantor's ID copy
☐ Guarantor's contact information

**Credit Report**:
☐ Credit report from UC (kreditupplysning.uc.se)
  - Shows you have no payment remarks
  - Costs ~200 kr
  - Valid for 1 month
  - Heimstaden/private landlords may request

## Writing Winning Applications

### Bostad Uppsala Application

**What to Know**:
- Applications are mostly automated
- Your message doesn't affect selection (queue days do)
- BUT: Good message shows you're serious

**Template**:
```
Hej,

I am interested in applying for [Address, Apartment Type].

I am [currently a student at Uppsala University / working as X / etc.] 
and am looking for long-term housing in Uppsala.

I have [X] queue days and am fully prepared to move in immediately upon acceptance.

Thank you for considering my application.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Contact Information]
```

**Key Points**:
✅ Short and professional
✅ Mention queue days (shows you understand system)
✅ Emphasize readiness to move (landlords like this)
✅ Include contact info

### Heimstaden Application

**What to Know**:
- First-come, first-served
- They check income, credit, references
- Professional presentation matters

**Template**:
```
Subject: Application for [Address] - Immediate Availability

Dear Heimstaden Housing Team,

I am writing to apply for the [1-room apartment] at [Address] listed on [Date].

About Me:
- [Working professional at X company / Graduate student at Uppsala University]
- Employed since [Date] with stable income of [X kr/month]
- Clean credit history (no payment remarks)
- Excellent references from previous landlords and employer

Income Qualification:
- Monthly income: [X kr] (see attached payslips)
- Required income (3x rent): [Y kr]
- I meet/exceed the income requirement

I am available to view the apartment at your earliest convenience and can sign the lease 
and provide deposit immediately upon approval.

Attached Documents:
- Copy of ID
- Last 3 months payslips
- Employment contract
- Previous landlord reference
- Credit report from UC

I am a clean, quiet, and responsible tenant who takes excellent care of rental properties. 
I am looking for long-term housing in Uppsala and would be honored to be considered for 
this apartment.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email Address]
```

**Key Points**:
✅ Professional but personable
✅ Immediately address income requirement (show you qualify)
✅ List attached documents (shows preparedness)
✅ Emphasize reliability and long-term intent
✅ Available for viewing ASAP (shows seriousness)
✅ Mention specific apartment (not generic copy-paste)

### Private Landlord Application

**What to Know**:
- Landlord choosing subjectively
- They want "good tenant" = clean, quiet, pays on time, doesn't cause problems
- Personal connection helps

**Template**:
```
Subject: Apartment Inquiry - Reliable, Long-Term Tenant

Hi [Landlord Name],

I saw your listing for the [apartment description] at [Address] and I'm very interested!

A bit about me:
I'm a [age]-year-old [profession/student] who recently moved to Uppsala for [work/studies]. 
I'm looking for a long-term rental where I can settle in and make a home.

I'm a quiet, clean, and responsible person - I take great care of rental properties and 
have excellent references from previous landlords. I'm employed at [company] / studying [program] 
and have stable income to comfortably afford the rent.

What I can offer as a tenant:
- Stable income (can provide payslips/employment contract)
- Clean credit history
- Excellent references
- Immediate availability
- Long-term commitment (not looking to move after a few months)

I'd love to view the apartment at your convenience - I'm flexible with timing and can meet 
anytime this week.

If you'd like to discuss further or need any additional information, please don't hesitate 
to contact me.

Thank you so much for considering my application!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email Address]
```

**Key Points**:
✅ Friendly, personable tone (not overly formal)
✅ Brief personal introduction (humanizes you)
✅ Emphasize being "good tenant" (clean, quiet, reliable)
✅ Mention long-term intent (landlords prefer stable tenants)
✅ Show enthusiasm (landlords want tenants who care)
✅ Flexible for viewing (shows commitment)

## Viewing Preparation

### Before the Viewing

**Research**:
☐ Google Maps: Location, nearby services
☐ Bus routes: Which lines, frequency
☐ Area reputation: Ask locals, check forums
☐ Comparable rents: Is this fair pricing?

**Prepare Questions**:
☐ When is move-in date?
☐ What's included in rent? (Water, heat, internet?)
☐ Electricity cost estimate?
☐ Laundry facilities? (Free or paid?)
☐ Parking available? (If you have car)
☐ Pets allowed? (If you have pet)
☐ Length of lease?
☐ Deposit amount?
☐ Notice period for moving out?

**Bring to Viewing**:
☐ ID (in case landlord wants to verify)
☐ Notebook and pen (take notes)
☐ Phone (photos if allowed)
☐ Printed copy of application/documents (shows preparedness)
☐ Questions list

### During the Viewing

**Inspect Thoroughly**:
☐ Check water pressure (turn on taps)
☐ Flush toilet (ensure it works)
☐ Check heating (radiators warm?)
☐ Open/close windows (do they work? Drafty?)
☐ Check electrical outlets (bring phone charger to test)
☐ Inspect walls/ceiling for moisture damage
☐ Check kitchen appliances (stove, fridge work?)
☐ Test locks on doors/windows
☐ Look for signs of pests (droppings, etc.)
☐ Check storage space (closets, basement access)
☐ Ask about noise (neighbors, street, parties)

**Red Flags**:
🚩 Mold or water damage (health hazard)
🚩 Broken heating (expensive, uncomfortable)
🚩 Signs of pests (mice, bedbugs)
🚩 Landlord evasive about questions
🚩 Contract terms unclear
🚩 Pressure to decide immediately without reading contract

**Make Good Impression**:
✅ Arrive on time (5 minutes early is perfect)
✅ Dress presentably (not formal, but clean/neat)
✅ Be polite and friendly
✅ Show genuine interest (positive comments about apartment)
✅ Ask thoughtful questions (shows you're serious)
✅ Don't be overly critical (even if you see issues - mention politely)
✅ Thank landlord for their time

### After the Viewing

**If Interested**:
☐ Send thank-you email within 24 hours
☐ Reiterate your interest
☐ Confirm you can meet any requirements
☐ Offer to provide additional documents if needed

**Example Thank-You Email**:
```
Subject: Thank You - [Address] Viewing

Hi [Landlord Name],

Thank you so much for showing me the apartment at [Address] today. I really appreciated 
your time and the opportunity to see the place.

After viewing, I'm very interested in moving forward with the application. The apartment 
is exactly what I'm looking for, and the location is perfect for [my work/studies].

As discussed, I can provide [any requested documents] at your convenience, and I'm ready 
to sign the lease and pay the deposit as soon as everything is finalized.

Please let me know if you need any additional information from me. I look forward to 
hearing from you!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Contact Info]
```

## Increasing Your Chances

### Strategy #1: Volume

**Apply Widely**:
- Don't wait for "perfect" apartment
- Apply to everything you'd accept
- More applications = more chances
- Bostad: No limit on applications (apply to 20+ per week!)

**Track Your Applications**:
Create spreadsheet:
| Date | Address | Type | Rent | Queue Days Needed | Status |
|------|---------|------|------|-------------------|--------|
| 2024-01-15 | Studentstaden 14 | 1-room | 6,500 kr | 700 | Applied |
| 2024-01-16 | Eriksberg 22 | 1-room | 5,800 kr | 600 | Viewing scheduled |

**Benefits**:
✅ Don't miss applications
✅ Track progress
✅ Learn patterns (what works, what doesn't)

### Strategy #2: Speed

**Set Up Alerts**:
- Bostad Uppsala: Email notifications for new listings
- Heimstaden: Check website daily or multiple times per day
- Blocket: Saved search with email alerts
- Facebook: Notifications on housing groups
- Set Google Alert: "Uppsala lägenhet" + "Uppsala apartment"

**Response Time Targets**:
- Bostad: Within 24 hours (queue determines winner, but apply early)
- Heimstaden: Within 1-3 hours (first-come advantage)
- Blocket/Second-hand: Within 30-60 minutes (speed critical)

**How to Be Fast**:
✅ Check emails/sites multiple times daily
✅ Have documents pre-prepared (copy-paste, attach, send)
✅ Use phone app (respond even when not at computer)
✅ Have template messages (customize quickly)

### Strategy #3: Flexibility

**Be Open To**:
- Corridor rooms (shared facilities) as stepping stone
- Suburbs (cheaper, faster access)
- Slightly higher rent than ideal (if affordable)
- Smaller apartment than ideal
- Unfurnished (you furnish yourself)
- Ground floor (some avoid, less competition)
- No balcony (some skip these)

**Say "Yes" to Good-Enough**:
- Perfect is enemy of good
- Getting IN is more important than perfect first apartment
- You can always upgrade later (keep applying while living there)

### Strategy #4: Networking

**Tell Everyone**:
- University classmates ("Anyone know of housing?")
- Coworkers
- Professors (PhD students often know housing)
- Facebook friends
- Nation members
- Gym/hobby acquaintances

**Word-of-Mouth Advantage**:
- Some landlords never publicly list
- Personal referral = trust
- Less competition (not on public market)

**How to Network**:
✅ Join Uppsala student Facebook groups (introduce yourself, mention need housing)
✅ Attend nation events (social + housing connections)
✅ Ask university housing office (they sometimes have leads)
✅ Talk to people in cafés, libraries (students = know housing scene)

### Strategy #5: Timing

**Best Times to Apply**:
- **June-August**: Most turnover (students graduating, people moving)
  - More competition BUT more listings
  - Apply aggressively
  
- **January-February**: New year moves
  - Moderate competition
  - Good time if you have decent queue days
  
- **September-December**: Slowest period
  - Fewer listings BUT less competition
  - Your queue days "worth more" (fewer applicants)
  - Hidden opportunity

**Worst Times**:
- **Late August**: Students desperate for housing (highest competition)
- **April-May**: Few listings, few movers

### Strategy #6: Presentation

**Professional Image**:
- Use professional email (firstname.lastname@gmail.com, not partyking420@hotmail.com)
- Proofread messages (no typos, grammar errors)
- Attach documents as PDFs (more professional than phone photos)
- Use clear file names (Firstname_Lastname_Payslips.pdf, not IMG_4839.jpg)

**Photo (If Landlord Asks)**:
- Clean, presentable appearance
- Smile (friendly, approachable)
- Simple background (not party photos!)
- Professional but not stiff

### Strategy #7: Build Queue Days

**Never Stop Accumulating**:
- Even if happy where you are, stay registered
- Queue days = future opportunities
- Costs nothing to maintain registration
- Insurance policy for future moves

**For Students**:
- Register for Bostad immediately (even if using student housing)
- Graduate with 1,000-1,500 days = smooth transition
- Many students miss this opportunity - don't be one of them!

## Common Mistakes & How to Avoid

### Mistake #1: Waiting for Perfect Apartment

**The Mistake**:
"I'll only apply to 2-room in Centrum"

**Why It's Bad**:
- May never qualify (queue days too short)
- Lose time accumulating
- Stay in expensive/temporary housing

**The Fix**:
✅ Apply to "good enough" apartments
✅ Get IN the system
✅ Upgrade later with more queue days

### Mistake #2: Slow Response

**The Mistake**:
See listing, think "I'll apply tomorrow"

**Why It's Bad**:
- Heimstaden: Already gone (someone applied in 1 hour)
- Second-hand: 50 people applied before you
- You lose to faster applicants

**The Fix**:
✅ Apply within HOURS
✅ Have documents ready
✅ Check listings multiple times daily

### Mistake #3: Generic Applications

**The Mistake**:
Copy-paste same message to every landlord

**Why It's Bad**:
- Private landlords notice
- Shows you're not genuinely interested
- Less personal connection

**The Fix**:
✅ Customize each message (mention specific apartment)
✅ Add personal touch
✅ Show genuine interest

### Mistake #4: Poor Documentation

**The Mistake**:
"I'll send documents if they ask"

**Why It's Bad**:
- Delays response (you scramble to find documents)
- Looks unprepared
- Another applicant (prepared) wins

**The Fix**:
✅ Have ALL documents ready before applying
✅ Attach proactively (shows professionalism)
✅ Update documents regularly (expired payslips = unprofessional)

### Mistake #5: Unrealistic Expectations

**The Mistake**:
"I have 300 queue days, I'll get a 1-room in Centrum"

**Why It's Bad**:
- Waste time applying to impossible apartments
- Miss opportunities in realistic range
- Get discouraged and give up

**The Fix**:
✅ Check "Last offered to" numbers (realistic expectations)
✅ Apply within your queue day range
✅ Understand market (Centrum = 800-1500 days)

### Mistake #6: Giving Up Too Soon

**The Mistake**:
Apply to 5 apartments, don't win any, stop trying

**Why It's Bad**:
- Housing search is numbers game (apply to 50-100+)
- One "no" means nothing
- Persistence wins

**The Fix**:
✅ Treat it like a job (apply daily)
✅ Set goal (10 applications per week)
✅ Track applications (see progress)
✅ Don't take rejections personally (queue days, not you)

### Mistake #7: Ignoring Red Flags

**The Mistake**:
Desperate, accept apartment despite obvious problems (mold, sketchy landlord, illegal contract)

**Why It's Bad**:
- Health risks
- Legal issues
- Lose money (scam, illegal deposit)

**The Fix**:
✅ Inspect thoroughly
✅ Read contract carefully
✅ Research landlord (Google, reviews)
✅ Walk away if something feels wrong

## Dealing with Rejection

### It's Not Personal

**Reality**:
- Bostad: Automatic by queue days (nothing about you)
- Heimstaden: Often just timing (someone applied 10 minutes before you)
- Private: May be 50 applicants for 1 apartment

**Mindset Shift**:
- "I didn't win THIS apartment" ≠ "I'll never get an apartment"
- Each application is lottery ticket (more tickets = better odds)
- Rejection means move to next opportunity

### Track Your Progress

**Metrics to Watch**:
- Applications sent: 50, 100, 150...
- Viewings scheduled: 2, 5, 10...
- Offers received: 1, 2, 3...

**Perspective**:
- 1 offer from 50 applications = 2% success rate = NORMAL
- If you've applied to 10 apartments and haven't won, that's expected
- Keep going!

### Learn and Adjust

**After 20-30 Applications, Review**:
- Are you applying to realistic apartments? (queue days match?)
- Is your message professional?
- Are you responding fast enough?
- Are you including all documents?
- Do you need guarantor?

**Adjust Strategy**:
- If no viewings: Apply to more realistic apartments
- If viewings but no offers: Improve presentation/references
- If offers to wrong apartments: Refine target (rent, location)

## When You Win

### Accept Quickly

**Bostad/Heimstaden Deadlines**:
- Usually 48 hours to accept
- Don't delay (you lose apartment if you miss deadline)

**Before Accepting, Verify**:
☐ Rent amount correct
☐ Move-in date works
☐ Contract terms acceptable
☐ You can afford deposit + first month

### Read Contract Carefully

**Key Sections**:
- **Rent amount**: Exactly what you pay monthly
- **Deposit**: How much, when returned
- **Move-in date**: When you can move
- **Lease length**: 1 year, indefinite, etc.
- **Notice period**: How long before you can move out (usually 3 months)
- **What's included**: Water, heat, internet?
- **Responsibilities**: Who repairs what?
- **Rules**: Pets, smoking, subletting

**Red Flags**:
🚩 Unclear terms
🚩 Excessive deposit (more than 3 months rent)
🚩 No written contract (always get written!)
🚩 Landlord refuses to explain terms

**If Unsure**:
- Ask landlord to clarify
- Consult Hyresgästföreningen (tenant union)
- Don't sign if uncomfortable

### Prepare for Move-In

**Financial**:
☐ Pay deposit
☐ Pay first month rent
☐ Budget for furniture/moving
☐ Set up electricity contract
☐ Buy home insurance

**Logistics**:
☐ Schedule moving day
☐ Arrange transportation (van rental, friends with car)
☐ Order furniture (if unfurnished)
☐ Notify address change (Skatteverket, bank, employer, university)
☐ Forward mail

**Move-In Inspection**:
☐ Do thorough inspection with landlord
☐ Take photos of existing damage (protect your deposit)
☐ Document everything in writing (landlord signs)
☐ Keep photos + inspection report safe

## Key Takeaways

✅ **Prepare everything in advance**: Documents, templates, folder
✅ **Apply widely**: Volume increases chances (50-100+ applications normal)
✅ **Respond fast**: Speed matters (especially Heimstaden, second-hand)
✅ **Be flexible**: Good enough > waiting for perfect
✅ **Stay professional**: Proofread, attach documents, follow up
✅ **Network actively**: Tell everyone, join groups, attend events
✅ **Track progress**: Spreadsheet keeps you organized and motivated
✅ **Don't give up**: Persistence wins (it's a numbers game)
✅ **Build queue days**: Even if happy now, keep registration active
✅ **Inspect thoroughly**: Protect yourself from bad apartments/scams

**Final Encouragement**: Every single person who lives in Uppsala went through this process. It feels overwhelming, but it DOES work. Stay persistent, stay organized, and you WILL find your apartment. Good luck! 🏠
