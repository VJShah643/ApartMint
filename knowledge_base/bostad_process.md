# Uppsala Bostadsförmedling Application Process

## Overview
Uppsala Bostadsförmedling (commonly called "Bostad Uppsala") is the primary rental queue system for first-hand rental apartments in Uppsala. It operates on a queue day system where your position is determined by how many days you've been registered.

## Registration Process

### How to Register
1. Visit: bostad.uppsala.se
2. Click "Bli medlem" (Become a member)
3. Verify identity using BankID or email
4. Fill in contact information
5. Agree to terms and conditions

### Registration Details
- **Cost**: Free to register
- **Time to register**: 5-10 minutes
- **Requirements**: Swedish personal number (personnummer) or coordination number
- **International students**: Can register with coordination number or passport
- **Queue days start**: Immediately from registration date

### Important Notes
- Queue days accumulate automatically - 1 day per calendar day
- You keep accumulating even if inactive
- Queue days are NOT transferable or sellable
- You keep your queue days even after renting through Bostad

## The Queue Day System

### How It Works
- Every registered member earns 1 queue day per calendar day
- When you apply for an apartment, your queue days determine your priority
- The applicant with the most queue days wins the apartment
- In case of a tie, earliest registration timestamp wins

### Realistic Queue Day Requirements

**Central Uppsala Areas** (Centrum, Svartbäcken, Kåbo):
- 1-room apartment: 600-900 queue days (1.5-2.5 years)
- 2-room apartment: 900-1300 queue days (2.5-3.5 years)
- 3-room apartment: 1200-1800 queue days (3-5 years)

**Near-Central Areas** (Luthagen, Eriksberg, Flogsta):
- 1-room apartment: 400-700 queue days (1-2 years)
- 2-room apartment: 700-1000 queue days (2-3 years)
- 3-room apartment: 1000-1400 queue days (3-4 years)

**Suburban Areas** (Gottsunda, Valsätra, Stenhagen):
- 1-room apartment: 200-500 queue days (6 months-1.5 years)
- 2-room apartment: 500-800 queue days (1.5-2 years)
- 3-room apartment: 800-1200 queue days (2-3 years)

### Checking Your Chances
- Look at "Senast erbjuden till" (Last offered to) on each listing
- This shows the queue days of the person who got the apartment last time
- Use this as a realistic gauge of what you need

## Browsing Apartments

### Finding Listings
- Log in to bostad.uppsala.se
- Click "Lediga lägenheter" (Available apartments)
- All current vacancies are displayed
- Use filters: area, rooms, rent, size

### Listing Information
Each listing shows:
- Address and area
- Number of rooms and size (m²)
- Monthly rent
- Move-in date
- Landlord information
- "Last offered to" queue days

### Saving Favorites
- Click "Intresserad" (Interested) to apply
- You can apply to unlimited apartments
- Applications cost nothing
- Apply to everything you'd accept

## Application Process

### How to Apply
1. Find an apartment you want
2. Click "Intresserad" button
3. Application is registered immediately
4. No additional forms or documents needed at this stage

### Key Application Facts
- **Cost**: Free
- **Number of applications**: Unlimited
- **Application duration**: Stays active until apartment is rented
- **Changing your mind**: You can withdraw anytime

### After Applying
- Your application goes into a queue with other applicants
- You can see how many others have applied
- When apartment becomes available, highest queue days wins
- You'll get email/SMS notification if selected

## Selection and Contract

### If You Win
- Email/SMS notification within 24 hours of decision
- **You have 48 hours to respond** - very important!
- Schedule a viewing (often same-day)
- If you accept, proceed to contract signing

### Contract Signing
**Required Documents**:
- Valid ID (passport or Swedish ID)
- Proof of income (employment contract, student aid confirmation, payslips)
- Bank statements (showing financial stability)
- References (optional but helpful)

**Income Requirements**:
- Recommended: Monthly income = 3x monthly rent
- Example: 8,000 kr rent requires ~24,000 kr/month income
- Student aid (CSN) counts as income
- Guarantors accepted for students

**Deposit**:
- Usually 1-3 months rent as security deposit
- Paid before move-in
- Returned when you move out (minus any damages)

### Contract Type
- **First-hand contract** (förstahandskontrakt)
- Direct with landlord
- Full tenant rights
- Unlimited duration (permanent until you terminate)
- You can sublet with permission

## Success Strategies

### Maximize Your Chances

**Start Early**:
- Register TODAY even if you're not moving for years
- Queue days are the most valuable currency
- Every day counts

**Be Realistic**:
- Check "Last offered to" on similar apartments
- Don't only apply to dream apartments requiring 1000+ days
- Apply to achievable options

**Cast a Wide Net**:
- Apply to ALL apartments you'd accept
- Don't limit yourself to one area
- Consider suburbs - faster access, cheaper rent

**Stay Active**:
- Check website weekly for new listings
- Respond immediately to notifications
- Keep your profile and contact info updated

**Timing Matters**:
- Most apartments posted: June-August (academic year turnover)
- Least competition: December-January
- Be ready with documents year-round

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Waiting to register** - Biggest mistake! Register now.

❌ **Only applying to central areas** - Suburbs are faster to access

❌ **Being too picky** - Apply to everything within your criteria

❌ **Missing the 48-hour response** - Set up notifications!

❌ **Not having documents ready** - Prepare income proof in advance

❌ **Ignoring "Last offered to"** - Be realistic about your queue days

❌ **Forgetting to update profile** - Keep contact info current

## Alternative Options for Low Queue Days

If you have fewer than 400 queue days:

**Other Queue Systems**:
- Heimstaden (no queue, application-based)
- Private landlords (Facebook groups, Blocket)
- Student housing organizations (separate queues)

**Second-Hand Contracts**:
- Sublets from first-hand tenants
- Shorter duration (max 2 years usually)
- Found on Blocket, Facebook, university boards
- Less tenant protection but faster access

**Corridor Rooms**:
- Shared kitchen/bathroom facilities
- Lower queue day requirements (200-400 days)
- Cheaper rent
- Good student option

## Important Tips for Students

### Student-Specific Advantages
- CSN (student aid) counts as income
- Many landlords student-friendly
- Guarantors (föräldrar) widely accepted
- Student unions offer housing support

### Student Housing Alternatives
- **Studentstaden**: Separate application system
- **Uppsala Studentbostäder**: Another student queue
- **Nation housing**: Through student nations
- **University guaranteed**: First-year international students

### Financial Planning
- Budget for 1-2 months rent upfront (deposit)
- Add moving costs (2,000-5,000 kr)
- Consider furniture if unfurnished
- Set aside emergency fund (3 months rent)
